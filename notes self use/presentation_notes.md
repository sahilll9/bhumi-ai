# Bhumi AI - Presentation Notes

## Key Features to Highlight

### 1. Multi-Land Registration & Verification
*   **Problem**: Framers often own multiple fragmented plots of land with different Khasra numbers.
*   **Solution**: The platform allows a single farmer profile to link **multiple Khasra numbers** and property documents.
*   **How it works**:
    *   Each submission captures the specific Khasra ID and Geolocation.
    *   Documents/Photos are linked to that specific plot.
    *   This creates a comprehensive digital land bank for the farmer.

### 2. Vision-Powered Fraud Detection
*   **Feature**: Real-time analysis of uploaded land documents.
*   **Benefit**: Instantly identifies potential tampering or fakes using Groq's high-speed Vision models.
*   **Demonstration**: Upload a sample document in the Chatbot and show the "Fraud Analysis Report" with its "Confidence Score".
