# 🌾 Bhumi AI: Empowering Rural Intelligence

> "Solving the gap between government schemes and the farmers who need them most."

---

## 🚩 The Problem
1. **The Information Gap**: Millions of farmers are eligible for government schemes but have no idea they exist.
2. **Verification Hurdles**: Verifying land ownership manually is slow, prone to errors, and open to corruption.
3. **Data Blindness**: Local administrators often lack real-time data on village progress and crop health.

## 💡 The Solution: Bhumi AI
A unified AI-powered platform that digitizes rural land management and automates the welfare distribution process.

### 🌟 Core Value Pillars
1. **Self-Service Verification**: Farmers can register their property using just their phone. The AI matches their **Khasra Number** and location instantly.
2. **Smart Scheme Matching**: Instead of searching through hundreds of PDFs,Bhumi AI analyzes a farmer's profile (land size, crops, income) and lists the exact schemes they qualify for.
3. **Multilingual Support**: Language is no longer a barrier. Our AI chatbot speaks **Hindi, English, Marathi,** and more.
4. **Command Center for Admins**: Real-time dashboards show "Hot Spots" where farmers need the most help, tracking every verification as it happens.

---

## 🛠️ The Technology
*   **AI Engine**: Powered by **Groq Llama 3.2 Vision** for instant fraud detection and high-speed multilingual interaction.
*   **Scalable Architecture**: Built on a microservices model (React, Node.js, Python) to handle thousands of concurrent requests.
*   **Robust Data**: Backed by a high-performance PostgreSQL database.

## 📈 Future Roadmap
*   **Satellite Verification**: Using GIS data to verify crop health remotely.
*   **Direct Bank Integration**: Speeding up the distribution of PM-KISAN installments.
*   **Offline Support**: Mobile apps that work in areas with low connectivity.
